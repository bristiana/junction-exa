Meet Bogdan.

A master’s student from Romania, three weeks into life in Espoo. No Finnish personal ID yet, no Finnish bank, full price on every bus. The answers he needs sit on Finnish pages that change every year. A chatbot trained last year doesn’t know them. Vali reads them today.

Monday

“How do I open a bank account without a Finnish ID?”

He sends it on WhatsApp and puts the phone away. In the background Exa searches the live web, reads the banks’ and Migri’s pages as they are today, and Vali keeps only the sentences a quote on the page backs.

Half a minute later: the answer for his case, the key facts, the exact Danske Bank and Migri links, and the one thing to do next.

Why live search matters

The rules moved, and Vali noticed.

On 1 August 2025 Kela moved students from the general housing allowance to the student housing supplement, and students had to apply again. Migri updates its student permit queue almost every week. Vali reads the page as it is now and links the exact line, so you can check it yourself.

Source: kela.fi

Saturday

“Book a public sauna at Kulttuurisauna on Sunday at 17:30.”

Vali opens the real booking page in its own private browser on Modal and fills it. He gets a picture of the filled form with Approve and Stop. Nothing is sent before he taps Approve, and Vali never pays for him.

Plans change? Stop now, Continue later. Vali picks up where it stopped without asking again.
