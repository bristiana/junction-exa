New in Finland? Vali handles it.

The errand agent for your first year in Finland. Ask on WhatsApp. Vali reads today’s official pages with Exa, answers with the exact source, and fills the forms for you.

Log inGet the extension
  1. AskOn WhatsApp, here, or in the Chrome extension. Then get on with your day.
  2. ResearchIn the background, Exa reads today’s official pages. Every sentence is checked against a quote.
  3. FillVali fills the real form with your details, in a private browser on Modal.
  4. Your OKYou check the filled form and tap Approve. Nothing is sent before that.

What Vali handles

  • Bank and IDA bank account without a Finnish ID. Your personal identity code at DVV.
  • PermitsEU registration and residence permits at Migri, with the documents to bring.
  • MoneyKela benefits and the student housing supplement, for your situation.
  • Getting aroundThe HSL student discount and the journey planner, already filled in.
  • HousingHOAS and student flats. A daily watch tells you when a new one appears.
  • WeekendsA public sauna, a film, a court for Saturday. Booked for you, sent only on your OK.

Today’s rules, not last year’s.

Finnish rules change every year. On 1 August 2025 Kela moved students to the student housing supplement and they had to apply again. A chatbot trained before that doesn’t know. Vali reads the page as it is now and links the exact line.

Source: kela.fi

Built on Exa and Modal

Exa finds and checks every answer on the live web. Modal runs a private browser for the forms. You give the final OK, and Vali never pays for you.
