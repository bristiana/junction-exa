# Vali: slide content

Six slides. Title plus at most three short lines each.

## 1. New in Finland means forms

- DVV, Migri, Kela, HSL, housing: every errand is a form.
- The rules sit on Finnish pages that change every year.
- A chatbot trained last year does not know this year's rules.

## 2. Vali handles it

- Ask on WhatsApp, the web or the Chrome extension.
- Vali answers with the exact source and fills the real form.
- Daily watches tell you only what is new, like a new flat.

## 3. How Vali uses Exa

- Agent runs and Search find the answer on the live web.
- Contents re-reads each page and checks every sentence against a quote.
- Monitors watch daily. Search and Contents feed the form as it is filled.

## 4. Forms, and you approve

- A private cloud browser on Modal fills the real form.
- You get a picture with Approve and Stop. Nothing is sent before Approve.
- Payments, logins and CAPTCHAs stay with you. The extension never presses submit.

## 5. Proof

- 13 of 13 research errands answered, 24 of 26 facts confirmed on the page (92%).
- Median 25 s, $0.33 of Exa for all 13. Approve reached on 4 of 5 form sites.
- One real sauna booking from WhatsApp, confirmed by email.

## 6. Try it

- Sign in with the demo account (details in the form notes).
- Ask: "How can I open a bank account without a Finnish personal ID?"
- Ask: "Book a public sauna at Kulttuurisauna on Sunday at 17:30, 1 man", then Fill it for me.
